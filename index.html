Why the name Bengal Empire?

The name 'Bengal Empire' is inspired by the rich historical legacy of the Bengal region, known for its innovation, culture, and strength, much like the empire we aim to build in the open-source world. It symbolizes our goal to create a powerful, community-driven ecosystem that empowers developers globally, with a nod to Bengali heritage through projects like BanglaScript.

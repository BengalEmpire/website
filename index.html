Frequently Asked Questions

Why the name 'Bengal Empire'? +
The name 'Bengal Empire' is inspired by the historical glory of Bengal at its peak—renowned for its power, rich culture, and thriving global trade. Today, after colonial and political upheavals, Bengal has been divided, and Bangladesh emerged as an independent nation. The name serves as a tribute to the legacy and greatness of the original Bengal Empire.
How can I contribute to projects? +
We welcome contributions from developers of all skill levels! Check out our GitHub repositories, read the contribution guidelines, and feel free to open issues or submit pull requests.
